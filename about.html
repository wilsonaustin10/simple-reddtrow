Who We Are

Reddtrow Properties, LLC is a family-owned real estate investment company based in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. We specialize in buying houses directly from homeowners for cash, offering a fast and hassle-free alternative to traditional real estate transactions.

Unlike real estate agents who list your property and wait for buyers, we are the buyers. This means you can skip the repairs, showings, and uncertainty that come with listing on the market.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to help homeowners who need to sell quickly get a fair cash offer without the stress and complications of traditional home sales. Whether you're facing foreclosure, going through a divorce, dealing with an inherited property, or simply need to relocate fast, we're here to help.
